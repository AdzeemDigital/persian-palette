## What each class of claim can and cannot rest on

- **HEX values**: designer choices. S001/S002 show when a value equals a modern digital name. No source here shows that any HEX was measured from a historical object, and the project reports `measuredSpectra: 0`.
- **UNESCO references**: S003 confirms IDs 114, 115, 1106, 1346, 1372, 1397 and 603. Intangible (ICH) listings, World Crafts Council designations, and inscriptions after 2014 are unverified.
- **Chemistry**: S004/S005 can check that a formula or CAS number is correct for a named substance. They cannot show that the substance is in any Persian artefact.
- **Manuscripts**: none opened. Titles are judged only for existence and plausibility from general knowledge, which is labelled as such in the ledger.
